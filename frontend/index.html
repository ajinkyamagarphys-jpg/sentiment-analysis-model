How It Works

1. Overview

This tool analyzes the emotional tone of text using AI models.

Disclaimer: This is not a diagnosis or replacement for professional care.

2. Analysis Pipeline

3. BERT Sentiment Model

BERT is a language model that reads context around words to classify overall sentiment as positive, neutral, or negative.

Example: "I handled this better than I expected."

Output: positive (0.84 confidence)

4. Gemini Mental Health Analysis

Gemini adds a mental-health lens that maps language patterns to categories like stress, anxiety, loneliness, or normal state.

  • A Anxiety
  • D Depression
  • S Stress
  • L Loneliness
  • G Anger
  • N Normal
  • R Suicidal

Detection Path Example

"I feel overwhelmed"

Input tone -> pressure language -> stress / anxiety candidate -> final label

5. Privacy & Data

Conversation snippets are saved locally in SQLite for context-aware analysis.

Your data stays on your device.

6. Understanding Your Results

Higher confidence means the model sees a clearer language pattern.

Low confidence means mixed signals; read output as directional, not absolute.

If content includes self-harm intent, seek immediate professional or emergency support.

US & Canada: Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
